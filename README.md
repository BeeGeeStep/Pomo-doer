# Pomo-doer!

A high-efficiency, low-distraction productivity app, combining the time-boxing strategy of a Pomodoro timer with the organisation of a full-function To-Do list app. 

[Features](#features)

[How to use](#how-to-use)

[Info](#info)

## Features

A fully featured To-Do list that supports: 
- Task reordering - tasks in the wrong place? Just click and drag it to wherever you want it to be. 
- Checkboxes for which tasks you've finished!
- Subtasks - perfect for breaking your big main tasks into digestable chunks!
- Local-storage ready! Never fear about losing your tasks/progress when you accidentally shut down the window - it'll be right there waiting for you when you come back, safe and sound. 
- File I/O capabilities - save your current task list to disk, and load up any Pomodoer lists from file!

And a Pomodoro-style timer supporting: 
- Standard 25/5 break work-break style, with a 15 minute final break
- A progress bar, because who wants to read numbers to figure out how much time until your break?
- Skip button - because life happens, and sometimes you gotta resume your session after that last-minute meeting your boss assured you really _couldn't_ be an email.

Plus some bonus features, like: 
- Selectable themes!
- A help button, for when you're really not sure where to start with the app!

## How to use

Download the file to your local desktop, and open the 'Pomo-doer.html' file in your browser of choice.
